# Gather product intent

Preserve the requester's original wording in an intake record. Inspect available
project context and evidence before asking questions that the repository can answer.

Separate the observed problem from a proposed solution. Establish affected users,
desired outcomes, observable success signals, constraints, assumptions, non-goals,
ownership, and material unknowns. Do not manufacture evidence or convert preferences
into requirements.

Use `templates/intake.yaml` and `templates/intent.yaml` from the lifecycle package,
or the adopting project's compatible equivalents. Mark the intent `pending` until
the designated owner accepts it. Intent approval does not authorize implementation.

Stop before requirements work when the accountable outcome, affected users, or
decision owner cannot be established and the missing answer could materially
change the work.
