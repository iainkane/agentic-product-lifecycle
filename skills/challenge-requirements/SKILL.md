# Challenge requirements

Review the original intake as well as the derived intent and requirements. Report
findings without rewriting disputed requirements as though they were agreed.

Check for solution bias, missing user groups, unsupported evidence, unobservable
success measures, contradictory requirements, boundary and failure omissions,
unowned decisions, and applicable privacy, security, accessibility, compatibility,
or operational gaps from the project overlay.

Classify each finding as blocking, material, or advisory. Link it to the affected
requirement or intent field and state the decision needed. A clean review is evidence
for product approval; it is not approval itself.
