# Prepare a lifecycle handoff

Identify the change and exact candidate. State the current and requested next stage,
completed work, evidence, failures and skipped checks, risks, open questions, and
the precise decision or action requested.

Use `templates/handoff.yaml` or a compatible project template. Do not infer approval
from tests, silence, file presence, or a prior-stage decision. Do not turn a handoff
into authorization for merge, deployment, publication, external communication, or
live-data mutation.
